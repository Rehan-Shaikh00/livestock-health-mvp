Escalation Workflow

1. Farmer Report

Farmer or livestock owner reports sickness or mortality.

2. Paravet Inspection

Village worker visits the location and records field observations.

3. Risk Triage

Rule-based or AI-assisted triage evaluates the reported case.

4. Veterinary Escalation

High-risk cases are forwarded to the Veterinary Officer for further action and possible sample collection.
